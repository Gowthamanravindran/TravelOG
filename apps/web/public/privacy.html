Waitlist privacy

This short notice covers the waitlist only. The full privacy policy for the app will be published before launch.

  • What we collect: your email address and, if you choose, how many friends you usually travel with.
  • Why: to tell you when early access opens and to invite testers.
  • Where it's stored: in our database hosted in India (Mumbai). It is not visible to the public and is not sold or shared.
  • How long: until launch plus 12 months, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Your rights: ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time by replying to any email from us.
