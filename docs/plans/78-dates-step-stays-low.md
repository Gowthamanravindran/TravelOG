## Change
Creating a trip, the dates step used to raise the drawer to its highest stop (88 %), covering most of the globe. It now rests at the same low height as picking countries (45 %), so the globe stays in view between the steps. The calendar scrolls inside the drawer; the Next button stays at the bottom of what shows, and the drawer can still be dragged up. Plan 67's step heights are otherwise unchanged.
